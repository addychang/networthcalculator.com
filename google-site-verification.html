Google Site Verification

This page is used for Google Search Console verification.

Please add your Google Search Console verification meta tag to the head section of your main pages.

Instructions:

  1. Go to Google Search Console
  2. Add your property (networthcalculator.com)
  3. Choose HTML tag verification method
  4. Copy the meta tag and add it to the <head> section of your pages
  5. Submit your sitemap.xml to Google Search Console
